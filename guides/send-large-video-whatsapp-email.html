How to send a large video on WhatsApp (16 MB) or by email (25 MB)

WhatsApp videos are limited to about 16 MB and many email services to 25 MB. Here are your options for a clip that is bigger.

You have a clip you want to send, and WhatsApp or your mail app says it is too large. There are a few ways round it. Which one is best depends on whether the other person needs the original quality or just needs to watch it.

WhatsApp: about 16 MB for videos

WhatsApp compresses videos you send as a normal video and has a cap of about 16 MB for them. A long or high-quality clip may be trimmed or rejected. If the person needs the full-quality original, you can send the file as a document instead, which skips video compression; WhatsApp allows much larger documents, but the other person has to download the whole file. Limits change from time to time, so check the current figure in the app.

Email: 25 MB, and a catch

Gmail allows attachments up to 25 MB; other providers vary, so check yours. The catch is that email encodes attachments, which makes them about a third bigger on the way. A 20 MB video can therefore be refused by a 25 MB limit. Aim well under, for example 18 MB for a 25 MB limit.

Free ways to do it

  • Trim the clip in Photos (iPhone) or QuickTime Player (Mac).
  • Export at a lower resolution from QuickTime Player (File > Export As > 720p or 480p).
  • iPhone Mail. When you attach a video, Mail can offer smaller size options.
  • Share a link. iCloud Mail Drop and other cloud drives let you send a link to a large file instead of attaching it.

The disadvantage of presets is that you only learn the final size after exporting, so it can take several tries.

Frequently asked questions

What is the WhatsApp video size limit?

About 16 MB for videos sent as normal videos, which is what FitSize Video's WhatsApp preset targets. WhatsApp can change this, so use Custom if you see a different figure.

Why does my 24 MB video fail on a 25 MB email limit?

Email encoding makes attachments larger on the wire, so the effective limit is lower than the number shown. Aim lower, such as 18 MB.

Will quality suffer?

A smaller file means less detail. The shorter the clip, the better it looks at a given size. If a clip is too long for the limit, FitSize Video says so rather than producing something unwatchable.

Can I send the original instead?

On WhatsApp you can send it as a document. By email, use a link service such as iCloud Mail Drop or a cloud drive.
