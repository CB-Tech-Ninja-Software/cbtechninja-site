How to send a video on Discord without Nitro

Discord's free upload limit is small. Here is how to get a clip under 10 MB, or share it another way.

Free Discord accounts can upload files up to 10 MB at the time of writing. Discord changes its limits from time to time, so check the number Discord shows you before you start. A clip from a phone or a game recorder is often 20 MB to several hundred MB, so it has to be shrunk or shared another way.

The two things that decide file size

A video's size is roughly its bitrate multiplied by its length. At a given quality, a shorter clip is smaller. So the two levers are: make it shorter (trim) and make it less detailed (lower resolution or bitrate). A 15-second clip fits 10 MB comfortably at good quality. A 2-minute clip squeezed into 10 MB will look rough.

Free ways to do it

  • Trim it. Cut the clip down to the part that matters. On iPhone, open the video in Photos and tap Edit; on Mac, use QuickTime Player’s Edit > Trim.
  • Export at a lower resolution. In QuickTime Player on Mac, File > Export As offers 1080p, 720p and 480p. 720p is a good compromise for chat.
  • Record smaller in the first place. Game recorders and phones let you pick 1080p or 720p and 30 fps instead of 4K and 60 fps.
  • Share a link instead. Upload to a cloud drive or video site and paste the link. Discord will show a preview for many links, and there is no size limit to fight.

The trouble with exporting presets is that you cannot tell in advance whether the result is under 10 MB. You export, check, and repeat.

What to expect at 10 MB

As a rough guide, a short clip of 10 to 20 seconds can keep decent quality at 10 MB, while a clip of a minute or more will look noticeably softer. If it is too long to fit without turning to mush, trimming is the honest fix.

At the time of writing it is 10 MB, and FitSize Video's Discord preset uses that figure. Discord changes limits from time to time, so use Custom if yours is different.

What if my clip is too long to fit 10 MB?

FitSize Video tells you it is too long for that size, rather than handing you an unwatchable file. Trim the clip, or share a link instead.

FitSize Video outputs an MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio, which is a widely supported combination.
