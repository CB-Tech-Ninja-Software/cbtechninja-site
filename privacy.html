Privacy policy

FitSize and FitClip. Effective 25 November 2026.

Summary

FitSize and FitClip collect no data. All processing happens on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The apps have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers. We do not receive your files, their contents, or information about how you use the apps.

Your files

Files you open in the apps are processed on your device only. They are never uploaded or sent to us or to any third party. The apps save shrunk copies where you choose and leave your originals untouched.

Network

The apps do not need the internet to do their work and send no data over the network.

Children

Since no data is collected, no data is collected from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions? See the support page.
