FitSize Video privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026. Published by Christopher Beulah (CB Tech Ninja Software).

Summary

FitSize Video collects no data. Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded.

This policy covers FitSize Video on Mac, iPhone and iPad.

What we collect

Nothing. FitSize Video has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it has no network access, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else. We do not receive your files, their contents, or information about how you use the app.

Your files

Videos you pick are processed on your device only. They are never uploaded or sent to us or to any third party. FitSize Video writes a new, smaller MP4 where you choose and leaves your original untouched.

Permissions

On the Mac, FitSize Video runs in Apple's app sandbox and can open only the videos you pick in the open dialog and save where you choose in the save dialog. It has no broader access to your folders.

On iPhone and iPad, you pick a video with Apple's Photos picker or the Files picker, and FitSize Video receives only what you pick. If you choose to save the smaller video to your Photos library, the app asks for add-only Photos access. That lets it add the new video; it cannot read, list or change anything else in your library. You can decline and save through Files or the share sheet instead.

Temporary files

To work on a video, FitSize Video makes working copies in the app's temporary folder. On the Mac, temporary copies are removed when you save, start over or quit. On iPhone and iPad, they are removed when you start over or pick another file, and iOS also clears the app's temporary storage. They are never visible to other apps and never leave your device.

Apple

If you buy FitSize Video from the App Store, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We do not see your payment details or Apple ID. Apple's own handling of your data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Children

FitSize Video is rated 4+. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
