FitSize Video: what goes in, what comes out

A quick reference for the formats FitSize Video accepts, the MP4 it produces, and how it names and counts files.

Input

FitSize Video accepts MP4, MOV and M4V video, one file at a time. Drop it on the window or use Choose file.

Output

  • Always an MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio.
  • Saved wherever you choose, with -fit added to the name.
  • Your original file is never changed.

How size is counted

Sizes use decimal units: 1 MB is 1,000,000 bytes. A file under a limit by that count is also under it if a service counts 1 MB as 1,048,576 bytes, so you are safe either way.

What it doesn't do

  • No trimming, editing or effects.
  • No batch mode: one video at a time.
  • No network access, no accounts and no analytics.

Presets

Discord (10 MB), WhatsApp (16 MB), Email (25 MB) and Custom. The presets are a convenience; services change their limits, so check your destination's current limit and use Custom when it differs. For Gmail, see email attachment limits.

System requirements

macOS 14, iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later. FitSize Video is coming soon to the App Store for Mac, iPhone and iPad.
