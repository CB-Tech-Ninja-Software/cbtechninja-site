jBudget vs. jBudget Online: which do you need?

Two apps, one family. Here is how to choose.

In one line each

  • jBudget is a single-device debt payoff planner that runs entirely offline with a locally encrypted database. It is available today.
  • jBudget Online is the household version, with end-to-end encrypted sync so couples and families can share one budget across phones and desktops. It is in pre-launch testing.

Choose jBudget if

  • One person on one device is making the plan.
  • You want something you can use today.
  • You want zero network connections.

Choose jBudget Online if

  • More than one person or device needs the same budget.
  • You want edits to merge across devices, including after weeks offline.
  • You can wait for the launch: it isn't downloadable yet and pricing hasn't been announced.

What they share

Both keep your data in an encrypted database on your own device, and neither contains analytics or ads. They have separate privacy policies: jBudget and jBudget Online.
