What the jBudget Online server can and can't see

The complete list, taken from the privacy policy, of what the sync relay stores.

What the relay can see

  • Your email address, collected when you sign in with Google to authorise sync. It is used to know which budgets you belong to. It is not used for marketing, not sold and not shared.
  • Sync metadata: a random ID for each device, timestamps, sequence numbers and the sizes of encrypted blobs.

What it can't see

Your accounts, balances, bills, debts, goals and transactions. They are encrypted on your device before they leave it, so the relay holds only ciphertext it cannot decrypt.

What else the app does

  • No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs, and no telemetry.
  • If you never turn on sync, the app makes no connection to our servers.
  • If you choose Google Drive backup instead of the relay, your encrypted data goes to a hidden app-data folder in your own Drive.

Deleting your data

Email the support address from the Google account you sync with and your email, sync metadata and encrypted blobs will be deleted from the servers within 30 days, per the policy. Removing the app from all devices also destroys the keys.

This page summarises the policy; the policy itself is the authoritative text.
