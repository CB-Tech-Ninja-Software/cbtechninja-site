Calculators lie by omission

Free payoff calculators tell you a plan will work. They show you'll be debt-free in 42 months and save $8,000 in interest. But they don't ask: when exactly do you have the money?

  • They average your income and bills into a tidy monthly number
  • They miss the week your insurance bill lands the same day as a card payment
  • They never notice your account would go to -$50 in real life
  • Subscription apps add bank sync and spending tracking, but still skip the cash-flow question

jBudget doesn't calculate. It simulates. You enter your accounts, income, bills, and debts. jBudget runs your financial life forward day by day and shows you when you'll be debt-free (if the plan is fundable), the total interest you'll pay, a month-by-month account activity table with every transaction and balance, and any dates when an account would run short, and by exactly how much.

The shortfall warning

A plan can look perfect on paper—a nice debt-free date, a clean interest total—and still fail the moment a real bill hits the wrong week. jBudget is the only planner that checks for that on purpose, and tells you before it happens.

  • Exact date, exact amount. Not “sometime this quarter”: the specific day an account would go negative, and by how much.
  • Caught before it matters. See it while you're building the plan, not after a real payment bounces.
  • Tells you what to try next. Free up more monthly funds, shift which account a bill draws from, or double-check a starting balance.
  • Adjust and re-check instantly. Change one number and the whole day-by-day simulation reruns.

Same plan, tighter numbers: Everyday Checking runs dry on 8/12/2026. jBudget names the account, the date, and what to check first.

Up and running in minutes

You'll need your account balances, income frequency, recurring bills, and debt details.

  1. Add your accounts: checking, savings, money market. jBudget treats each separately.
  2. Add your income: salary, gig work, tax refunds. Specify the amount and how often.
  3. Add your bills: rent, utilities, subscriptions, insurance. jBudget handles weekly, monthly, quarterly, annual, or custom dates.
  4. Add your debts: credit cards, loans, mortgages. Enter the balance, interest rate, and minimum payment.
  5. Run your plan: jBudget simulates months forward and shows whether the plan works.

Features

  • Simulates day by day. Every paycheck, bill and debt payment lands on the actual date it's scheduled. If an account runs dry before a payment is due, jBudget tells you exactly which day and by how much.
  • Shortfall warning. The exact date and amount an account would go negative, caught while you build the plan, with suggestions on what to try next.
  • Completely offline. Your data never leaves your device: no cloud sync, no bank connection, no account to create.
  • Locally encrypted. Your financial database lives encrypted on your own device.
  • Account activity table. A month-by-month view of every transaction and running balance across all your accounts.
  • Promotional APR warnings. Warns you when a 0% APR promotion is about to expire.
  • Confirm queue. Income and bills are projected forward but marked unconfirmed until they actually happen.
  • One-time purchase. Start free with up to 4 debts and 4 goals. Unlock everything for a single $0.99 payment: no subscription, no ads.
