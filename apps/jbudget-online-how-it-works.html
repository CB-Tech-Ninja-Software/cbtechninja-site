How jBudget Online works

How a household shares one budget while the server only ever sees ciphertext.

1. It starts on your device

Your accounts, balances, income, bills, debts, savings goals and transactions live in an encrypted database (SQLCipher, AES-256) on each of your own devices. Using the app doesn't require an account with us, and if you never turn on sync it makes no connection to our servers.

2. A pairing phrase makes the keys

To share a budget, devices are paired with a pairing phrase. Encryption keys are derived from it, and neither the phrase nor the keys leave your devices.

3. Encrypted before it leaves

Changes are encrypted with AES-256-GCM on your device. What travels is unreadable ciphertext, so we can't decrypt your budget and neither can anyone at the hosting provider.

4. Relay or your own Google Drive

Sync can go through a relay we operate on Cloudflare's platform, or through a hidden app-data folder in your own Google Drive. In both cases the content stays end-to-end encrypted. The relay can see your sign-in email and sync metadata (device IDs, timestamps, sequence numbers, blob sizes) and nothing about your finances.

5. Offline for weeks, converging cleanly

Devices can edit offline for weeks. When they reconnect, their changes converge rather than overwrite each other.

6. Deleting is real deletion

Uninstalling removes the local database. You can ask us to delete your email, sync metadata and encrypted blobs from the relay, and because only your devices hold the keys, removing the app from all devices also makes any remaining ciphertext permanently unreadable.
