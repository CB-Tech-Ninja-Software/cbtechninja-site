Supervision percentage maths: what 5% and 10% mean in hours

A worked example of how FieldMate's built-in pathway settings translate into hours, so you can sanity-check your own log.

The settings FieldMate ships with

PathwayTotal hoursSupervision share
Standard2,0005%
Concentrated1,50010%

The thresholds are configurable in the app, so you can update them if the BACB changes them.

The arithmetic

  • 5% of 2,000 hours is 100 hours.
  • 10% of 1,500 hours is 150 hours.
  • Supervision percentage is supervised hours divided by total hours logged, times 100. For example, 74 supervised hours out of 1,000 logged is 7.4%.

FieldMate's dashboard shows the percentage so you don't have to calculate it, and checks the ratio against every entry.

What this guide doesn't say

How the BACB applies a supervision percentage (for example, over what period it is measured) is for the BACB to say. Read its fieldwork page and the BCBA Handbook, and confirm with your supervisor, before relying on any number here.
