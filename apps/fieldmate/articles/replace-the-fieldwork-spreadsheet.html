Replacing the shared fieldwork spreadsheet

A spreadsheet works until the night before a review. Here is what a purpose-built log does differently.

Where spreadsheets struggle

  • Percentages are formulas you maintain yourself, and one broken cell can skew the total.
  • Supervised and independent hours sit in separate columns that are easy to misalign.
  • The file is often shared, so your BACB ID, address and hours are in someone else's account.
  • Producing a clean report means reformatting at the last minute.

What a purpose-built log does

  • Knows the two pathways. Standard (2,000 hours, 5% supervision) or Concentrated (1,500 hours, 10%), with thresholds you can change.
  • Does the ratio maths and checks the supervision percentage against every entry.
  • Keeps data on your device, with an optional PIN or biometric lock.
  • Exports on demand as a dated PDF or CSV, split by domain and hour type.

Moving over

Start logging new entries in the app and keep your spreadsheet as the record of past hours, or re-enter older entries if your supervisor needs one combined report. Export a CSV from FieldMate whenever you want to see the data in a spreadsheet again.

Keep a backup either way

Whatever you use, keep an independent copy. FieldMate's own guidance is to keep an exported PDF or CSV, or use the optional Google Drive backup, in addition to the local database.
