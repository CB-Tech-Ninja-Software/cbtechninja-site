Exporting and sharing your hours report

How to get a dated PDF or CSV out of FieldMate, and what stays private when you share it.

Steps

  1. Open the Export screen. It shows a monthly summary preview.
  2. Choose PDF report or CSV spreadsheet.
  3. Share it with your device's share sheet (email, messaging or cloud storage).

What's in it

Reports are dated and split by domain and hour type. They include the information you've entered, such as your name, BACB ID, hours and supervisor details, so your supervisor can verify them.

What stays private

  • Reports are generated entirely on your device.
  • Sharing is your choice: the developer never receives a copy.
  • FieldMate has no analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash-reporting code.

Use exports as backups

FieldMate's privacy policy recommends keeping an independent backup of your data, such as an exported PDF or CSV, or the optional Google Drive sync, in addition to the local database. Export at the end of each month and you'll always have a recent snapshot.
