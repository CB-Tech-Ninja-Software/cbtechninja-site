What a fieldwork log entry should contain

Your supervisor has to verify every entry. Capturing the same details each time makes that quick.

The details FieldMate records per entry

  • Date and time of the fieldwork.
  • Domain, one of the nine (A through I) FieldMate tracks.
  • Supervised or independent.
  • Hours, split into restricted and unrestricted time.
  • For supervised entries: observation minutes and the supervision format.
  • Supervisor and employer, attached from the profile you set up once.

Why consistency matters

A supervisor reviewing months of entries is looking for gaps: a missing supervisor, an entry with no domain, hours that look like minutes. Entering the same fields every time, immediately after the work, is the cheapest way to avoid reconstructing a month from memory.

The minutes-versus-hours trap

Observation is recorded in minutes and hours in hours, so a quick entry can swap them. FieldMate asks you to confirm before saving, so a slip doesn't quietly change your totals.

Check the rules that apply to you

This guide describes what FieldMate records, not the BACB's rules. Requirements vary with your certification timeline; the BACB's site notes that its 2027 requirements may change the fieldwork rules and says to confirm which version applies to your start date. Check the BACB's fieldwork page and handbook, and ask your supervisor.
