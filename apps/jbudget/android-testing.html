Android testing on Google Play

Join the jBudget Android test

jBudget for Android is still in testing, so it is not in the public Google Play listing yet. Follow these steps to try it early and help us make it better.

What testing means

  • Free early access. You get jBudget on your Android phone before it is publicly listed on Google Play.
  • It may have bugs. Things can look odd or break. That is the point of a test.
  • Your feedback helps. Tell us what confuses you or what goes wrong and we will fix it.

What you need

  • An Android phone or tablet with the Google Play Store.
  • A Google account (the one you are signed in with on the Play Store).

How to join, step by step

  1. Join the tester group. Open the jBudget testing Google Group and tap “Join group”, signed in with your Google account.
  2. Opt in on Google Play. Open the opt-in page and tap “Become a tester”. If Google says the test is not available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.
  3. Install jBudget. Open jBudget on Google Play (or tap the install link on the opt-in page) and tap Install.
  4. Open it and try it. Add a few debts and goals, and tell us how it goes.

Tip: use the same Google account in every step. Google only lets the account you signed up with install the test version.

What happens next

  • jBudget is $0.99 one-time on other platforms, with a free start tier. We will tell testers if anything about pricing changes on Android.
  • Updates arrive automatically through the Play Store, like any other app.
  • To leave the test, open the same opt-in link and choose “Leave the test”, or just uninstall the app. You can rejoin any time.

Send feedback

Found a bug or have an idea? Email jbudgetsupport@gmail.com. It helps to tell us your phone model and what you were doing when it happened. Screenshots are welcome, but please leave out anything private.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Joining the test costs nothing.

Will I lose my data when the test ends or I leave?

Your data lives on your phone. Leaving the test does not erase it, but uninstalling the app does. If the app offers a backup or export, use it before you uninstall.

I opened the link and Google says it is not available.

Check that you are signed in with the same Google account you used to join, and that you finished every step above. Then wait a few minutes and try again. If it still fails, email us with the Google account you used.

Why a Google Group?

Google Play checks the group to know who may install the test version. Joining the group is how you get on the list.
