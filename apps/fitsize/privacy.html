FitSize privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026. Published by Christopher Beulah (CB Tech Ninja Software).

Summary

FitSize collects no data. Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded.

This policy covers FitSize on Mac, iPhone and iPad.

What we collect

Nothing. FitSize has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it has no network access, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else. We do not receive your files, their contents, or information about how you use the app.

Your files

Photos and PDFs you pick are processed on your device only. They are never uploaded or sent to us or to any third party. FitSize saves the smaller copy where you choose and leaves your original untouched.

Permissions

On the Mac, FitSize runs in Apple's app sandbox and can open only the files you pick in the open dialog and save where you choose in the save dialog. It has no broader access to your folders.

On iPhone and iPad, you pick a photo with Apple's Photos picker or a file with the Files picker, and FitSize receives only what you pick. It does not ask for access to your whole photo library, and the smaller copy is saved through the Files save sheet or the share sheet.

Temporary files

To work on a file, FitSize makes working copies in the app's temporary folder. On the Mac these are removed when you save, start over or quit. On iPhone and iPad they stay in the app's own private temporary storage, which iOS clears automatically. They are never visible to other apps and never leave your device.

Apple

If you buy FitSize from the App Store, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We do not see your payment details or Apple ID. Apple's own handling of your data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Children

FitSize is rated 4+. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
