How to shrink a PDF for an upload portal

The form says 2 MB and your PDF is bigger. Here is how to get under the limit without losing the text, and what to know about the fallback.

Find the exact limit

The upload page usually states a limit, such as “PDF, maximum 2 MB”. Use that figure. FitSize's presets are 200 KB, 500 KB, 1 MB, 2 MB and 5 MB, and Custom accepts anything else, like 750 KB or 1.5 MB.

Steps

  1. Drop the PDF into FitSize (or click Choose file).
  2. Pick the limit.
  3. Click Fit. FitSize recompresses the images inside the PDF and keeps your text selectable.
  4. Read the result line. “Text kept, images compressed” means the text is still searchable. Click Save and upload that copy.

If the images aren't enough

A PDF that is mostly text is already small. A PDF that is large is usually large because of embedded images, such as scanned pages or photos. If recompressing those images can't reach your limit, FitSize can fall back to rebuilding each page as an image. The result screen tells you when this happens, because in that case the text in the smaller copy cannot be selected or searched. Page count and page size stay the same either way.

Some portals need selectable text, for example if they extract details from your document. If yours does, try a larger limit so the text-keeping method can succeed, or ask the portal's support whether a bigger file is allowed.

Before you upload

  • Open the shrunk copy and check every page is readable.
  • Keep the original: FitSize never overwrites it.
  • If it says the limit is too small, read what to do when a limit is too small.

The whole job happens on your device, so CVs, transcripts and ID scans never go to an online compressor.
