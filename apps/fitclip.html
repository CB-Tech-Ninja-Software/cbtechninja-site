The problem: the clip is bigger than the limit

You recorded a clip and the chat app, email or upload form says it is too large. Most video tools make you guess at bitrates and resolutions, then try again until it fits. FitSize Video works the other way round: you tell it the size you need, and it works out the rest.

  • Messaging and email services each cap how big a file can be, and the caps change from time to time.
  • Guessing at export settings wastes time, and you only find out it was wrong after the upload fails.
  • Online compressors need you to upload the video first, which you may not want to do.

How it works

  1. Drop a video onto the window, or click Choose file. MP4, MOV and M4V are accepted.
  2. Pick a limit: Discord (10 MB), WhatsApp (16 MB), Email (25 MB), or type your own size.
  3. Click Fit, watch the progress (you can cancel any time), then Save. Your original is never changed.

Behind the scenes, FitSize Video works out how much data your clip can use, re-encodes it as H.264 video with AAC audio in an MP4, then checks the real size of the result. If it comes out slightly over, it tries again at a lower bitrate. You get a file under your limit, or a clear message if it can't be done. Read the full walk-through.

Features

  • Target size first. You choose the limit; FitSize Video does the bitrate maths.
  • Checked, not estimated. The size of the finished file is measured, and it retries at a lower bitrate if it lands over.
  • Presets and custom. Discord (10 MB), WhatsApp (16 MB), Email (25 MB), or any size you type.
  • Honest about the trade-off. If a clip is too long for a limit, it says “too long for this size” instead of producing mush.
  • Cancel any time, with a progress bar while it works.
  • Private by design. No accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or network access.

Frequently asked questions

Does FitSize Video change my original video?

No. It writes a new MP4 copy, with -fit added to the name where you choose to save it. The original is never modified.

What video files can I drop in?

MP4, MOV and M4V. The result is always an MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio.

What if my clip is too long to fit the limit?

FitSize Video tells you it is too long for that size rather than handing you something unwatchable. Pick a larger limit, or use a shorter clip.

Does anything get uploaded?

No. FitSize Video has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or network access, and your videos are processed on your device. On iPhone it asks only for add-only Photos access, to save the smaller video. See the privacy policy.

Are the Discord, WhatsApp and Email presets guaranteed to match the services?

No. The presets (Discord 10 MB, WhatsApp 16 MB, Email 25 MB) are a convenience. Services change their limits from time to time, so if yours is different, choose Custom and type your own size.

How much does it cost, and when is it out?

FitSize Video is submitted to the App Store, coming soon. $4.99, pay once.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes. The iPhone and iPad version is included when you buy it for the Mac (one purchase).

What does it need?

macOS 14, iOS 17 or iPadOS 17 or later.

Can it trim or edit my clip?

No. FitSize Video does not trim, edit or add effects, and it handles one video at a time. It only makes the file fit.
