jBudget Online

Household budgeting with zero-knowledge sync: couples and families share one budget across phones and desktops, and the server only stores ciphertext it cannot read.

Features

  • One shared household budget across phones and desktops.
  • End-to-end encryption. AES-256-GCM is applied on your device, and keys derive from a pairing phrase that never leaves your devices. The server stores only ciphertext it cannot decrypt.
  • Encrypted on every device. Budget data lives in an encrypted database (SQLCipher, AES-256) on each of your own devices.
  • Offline-first. Devices can edit offline for weeks and converge cleanly when they reconnect.
  • Sync is optional. If you never turn on sync, the app makes no connection to our servers. Sync can use our relay or your own Google Drive app-data folder.
  • Platforms: Android and Windows.

Availability

In testing. jBudget Online is feature-complete and in pre-launch relay testing. It is not available to download yet, and there is no store listing to link to.
