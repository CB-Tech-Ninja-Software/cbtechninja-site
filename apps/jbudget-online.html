The problem: one household, several devices

A household budget lives on more than one phone or computer. Syncing usually means an account on someone's server holding your plain-text finances. jBudget Online keeps the household in step without that trade-off.

  • Everyone in the household sees the same accounts, bills and plan.
  • The server can't read your finances, even if it were stolen.
  • Edits made offline merge cleanly when devices reconnect.

How it works

  1. Budget on your own device. Data lives in an encrypted database (SQLCipher, AES-256) on each device. No account with us is needed to use the app.
  2. Optionally turn on sync. Pair devices with a pairing phrase. Keys are derived from it and never leave your devices.
  3. Changes are encrypted before they leave. AES-256-GCM is applied on your device; the relay or your Google Drive app-data folder stores only ciphertext.
  4. Devices converge. Edit offline for weeks and the devices merge cleanly when they reconnect.

Features

  • One shared household budget across phones and desktops.
  • End-to-end encryption. AES-256-GCM on your device; keys derive from a pairing phrase that never leaves your devices. The server stores only ciphertext it cannot decrypt.
  • Encrypted on every device. SQLCipher (AES-256) database on each of your own devices.
  • Offline-first. Devices can edit offline for weeks and converge cleanly.
  • Sync is optional. If you never turn on sync, the app makes no connection to our servers. Sync can use our relay or your own Google Drive app-data folder.
  • No analytics. No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs, and no telemetry.
  • Platforms: Android and Windows.

Frequently asked questions

Can I download jBudget Online?

Not yet. It is feature-complete and in pre-launch relay testing, and there is no store listing to link to.

Which platforms?

Android and Windows.

Can the server read my budget?

No. Data is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM using keys derived from your pairing phrase, which never leaves your devices. The relay stores only ciphertext it cannot decrypt.

What does the server know about me?

Per the privacy policy: the email address from Google sign-in (to know which budget you belong to) and sync metadata such as device IDs, timestamps, sequence numbers and encrypted blob sizes. No names, contacts, location or financial content.

Do I have to use sync?

No. If you never turn on sync, the app makes no connection to our servers. Sync can use our relay or your own Google Drive app-data folder.

Can I edit offline?

Yes. Devices can edit offline for weeks and converge cleanly when they reconnect.

How is it different from jBudget?

jBudget is the single-device debt payoff planner and is available today. jBudget Online is the household version, with encrypted sync across devices. See jBudget vs. jBudget Online.

What will it cost?

Pricing hasn't been announced.
