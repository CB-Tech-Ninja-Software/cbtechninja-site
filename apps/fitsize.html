FitSize

Make a PDF or photo fit the upload limit, without uploading it anywhere.

What it does

  • Drop a PDF or photo on the window.
  • Pick a limit, such as 200 KB, 2 MB or 5 MB, or type your own.
  • Get a copy that fits, with a before and after preview. Your original stays untouched.

For job, visa, school and government upload forms that say "file too large".

Privacy

Your files never leave your Mac. No accounts, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.

Availability

FitSize is in development and is not yet on the Mac App Store. Price and release date to be announced.
